📝 BPSC MAINS HIGH-YIELD REVISION NOTES (10 QUESTIONS)

📌 GENERAL STUDIES - 1 (GS-1)

🔹 Q1. Trace the expansion of Western & Technical Education in Bihar (1857–1947).

  • Hook / Core: Wood’s Despatch (1854) served as the base framework; shifted priorities toward creating an administrative workforce.
  • Phase I (1857–1900): Establishment of Patna College (1863); expansion of Zilla schools; Hunter Commission (1882); Bankipore Girls' High School (1892).
  • Phase II (1900–1920): Post-1912 separation; Patna University established in 1917; Patna Training College (1908).
  • Phase III (1920–1947): Medical setup at PMCH (1925); Agricultural research at Pusa (1905); Bihar Veterinary (1927); ISM Dhanbad (1926).
  • Nationalist Counter: Mahatma Gandhi & Dr. Rajendra Prasad established Bihar Vidyapeeth (1921) for indigenous technical skills.

🔹 Q2. Strategic Evolution & Significance of QUAD (2025–2026 Context).

  • Pillars: IPMDA (Maritime Domain Awareness) for dark shipping detection; Critical & Emerging Tech (CET) for supply-chain resilience.
  • Significance: Balances regional aggression without military alignment; reinforces India as the Net Security Provider in the IOR.
  • Nuance: Concerns over ASEAN centrality; continental boundary threats vs. maritime focus.

🔹 Q3. Statistical Analysis (Cropped Land Distribution & Acreage).

Data Base: Total Area = 1200 Acres ($360^\circ$); $1^\circ = 3.333\text{ acres}$.
  • (i) Acreage (Wheat + Maize): $(108^\circ + 54^\circ) = 162^\circ \rightarrow \mathbf{540\text{ Acres}}$.
  • (ii) Output Ratio (Rice : Wheat): $(300 \times 2.5) : (360 \times 3.2) = 750 : 1152 = \mathbf{125 : 192 \approx 0.651}$.
  • (iii) New Sugarcane Angle: Area rises to $300\text{ acres}$; Total rises to $1320\text{ acres} \rightarrow \mathbf{81.82^\circ}$.

📌 GENERAL STUDIES - 2 (GS-2)

🔹 Q4. "The Indian Parliament is not a sovereign legislature; it has vast but not unlimited powers."

  • Core Doctrine: Dicey's British Parliamentary Sovereignty vs. Indian Constitutional Supremacy.
  • Constitutional Restraints:
    • Written Constitution & 7th Schedule divisions.
    • Judicial Review (Article 13) against Fundamental Right violations.
    • Basic Structure Doctrine (Kesavananda, 1973) limiting Article 368.
    • Federal controls & Rajya Sabha representation.

🔹 Q5. Causes of Industrial Backwardness in Bihar & Remedies.

  • Historical Factors: Freight Equalization Policy (1952–1993); Bifurcation of 2000 (mineral base loss to Jharkhand); persistent low CD-Ratio.
  • Key Initiatives: Bihar Industrial Investment Promotion Policy (BIIPP); Biofuels & Ethanol Promotion Policy; Textile & Leather Policy.

🔹 Q6. S&T in Disaster Resilience (Bihar Floods & Droughts).

  • Forecasting: ISRO SAR Remote Sensing, Doppler radars, AI hydrodynamic flood models.
  • Response: Drone surveys and relief distribution; satellite emergency units.
  • Long-Term Resilience: Embankment stabilization using geo-textiles; flood-resilient paddy (Swarna Sub-1) and drought-tolerant seeds (Sahbhagi Dhan).

📌 GENERAL HINDI & ESSAY

🔹 Q7. सामान्य हिंदी (व्याकरण एवं मुहावरे)

  • ❌ श्री कृष्ण के अनेको नाम है। $\rightarrow$ ✅ श्री कृष्ण के अनेक नाम हैं।
  • ❌ बन्दूक एक बहुत ही उपयोगी शस्त्र है। $\rightarrow$ ✅ बन्दूक एक बहुत ही उपयोगी अस्त्र है।
  • ❌ मैं गाने की कसरत कर रहा हूँ। $\rightarrow$ ✅ मैं गाने का अभ्यास (या रियाज़) कर रहा हूँ।
  • अधजल गगरी छलकत जाए: अल्पज्ञानी द्वारा अत्यधिक दिखावा करना।
  • नाकों चने चबाना: अत्यधिक कठिनाई या संघर्ष का सामना कराना।

🔹 Q8. Essay: Democracy, Dissent, and Freedom of Expression.

  • Framework: Dissent as the core metric of democracy; Amartya Sen's *The Argumentative Indian*.
  • Balance: Article 19(1)(a) paired with objective checks under Article 19(2).
  • Synthesis: *"Dissent is the safety valve of democracy."* (Supreme Court of India).

📌 ECONOMICS OPTIONAL (UNITS I & II)

🔹 Q9. Samuelson’s Multiplier-Accelerator Interaction Model.

  • Equation: $Y_t = c(1 + v)Y_{t-1} - c \cdot v \cdot Y_{t-2} + G_0$
  • Outcomes: Depending on $c$ (MPC) and $v$ (Accelerator), leads to damped non-oscillatory, damped cyclic swings, or explosive cycles.

🔹 Q10. Fiscal Federalism & Resource Devolution (Bihar Context).

  • Vertical Imbalance: Growth of non-shareable Cesses & Surcharges shrinking net divisible pool under Article 270 below 41%.
  • Bihar Bottlenecks: Low Own Tax Revenue (OTR); over 70% dependence on central transfers; strict FRBM borrowing limits.
