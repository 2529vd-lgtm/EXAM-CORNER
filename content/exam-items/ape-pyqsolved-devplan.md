### December 2018

**Q1.** According to Schumpeter Theory of Development, which of the following statements is  
correct? *[December 2018 · Q.93]*

(1) The expansion of economic output is limited by the real savings of the economy.  
(2) Democracy enhances the efficiency of an economic system.  
(3) All the above are correct  
(4) None of the above are correct

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(4)** None of the above are correct  
Schumpeter: innovation is financed by bank credit, not limited by real saving; democracy is not part of the theory.

</details>

**Q2.** Technological dualism means *[December 2018 · Q.96]*

(1) Prevalence of different production functions in the industrial sector in underdeveloped countries  
(2) Prevalence of different technologies in agricultural sector in underdeveloped countries  
(3) Prevalence of different production functions in the advanced and traditional sectors in underdeveloped countries  
(4) Prevalence of different production functions in domestic and export sectors of underdeveloped countries

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(3)** Prevalence of different production functions in the advanced and traditional sectors in underdeveloped countries  
Technological dualism (Higgins): different production functions in the advanced and traditional sectors.

</details>
