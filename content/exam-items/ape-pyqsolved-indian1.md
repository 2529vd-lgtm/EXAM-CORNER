**Q1.** Which of the following are the objectives of the Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices  
(CACP)?  
(i) To ensure meaningful real income levels to the farmers  
(ii) To stabilise agricultural prices  
(iii) To protect the interest of the consumers

(2) (i) and (ii)  
(3) (ii) and (iii)

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(4)** All of the above  
CACP aims at fair farm incomes, price stability and consumer protection.
