## 2. History and culture (UP)
- Buddhist sites: **Sarnath** (first sermon), **Kushinagar** (mahaparinirvana), **Shravasti**, **Kaushambi**, Sankisa.
- Jain: Ayodhya (birthplace of five Tirthankaras), Shravasti, Kaushambi, Varanasi (Parshvanath).
- 1857: Meerut (start), Kanpur, Lucknow, Jhansi, Bareilly (see History notes).
- Freedom struggle in UP: Chauri Chaura (1922), Kakori (1925), Azad at Alfred Park (1931), Ballia parallel government (1942).
- **Kumbh Mela**: Prayagraj (**UNESCO intangible heritage, 2017**); **Maha Kumbh 2025** held at Prayagraj (13 Jan–26 Feb 2025).
- **Ram Mandir, Ayodhya**: consecration (pran pratishtha) **22 January 2024**.
- Folk dances: Charkula (Braj), Raslila, Nautanki, Khayal, Karma (tribal, Sonbhadra), Dhobiya, Jhoomar. Folk songs: Kajri (Mirzapur), Chaiti, Birha, Alha (Bundelkhand), Sohar.
- Crafts (GI): **Banarasi saree**, Lucknow **chikankari**, Moradabad brassware, Firozabad glass, Agra petha, Kannauj itr, Bhadohi carpets, Khurja pottery, Saharanpur woodcraft, Varanasi **Gulabi Meenakari**. UP has the **most GI tags** of any state (reported 75+).
- **ODOP** (One District One Product): launched **24 Jan 2018**.

## Exam traps
- UP got its present name on **24 January 1950**; UP Diwas is celebrated on 24 January.
- State bird is the sarus crane; state animal is the barasingha (swamp deer).
- Sucheta Kripalani (UP) was India's first woman Chief Minister; Sarojini Naidu (UP) the first woman Governor.
- Kumbh Mela (2017) is on UNESCO's intangible heritage list.
- UP has 75 districts and 18 divisions.
