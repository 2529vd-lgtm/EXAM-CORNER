> **Geography: basic to advanced.** About 20 questions in UPPCS prelims: physical geography, India, world, and Uttar Pradesh.

## 1. Physical geography (basics)
### Earth and interior
- Layers: crust (SIAL, SIMA), mantle, core. Discontinuities: **Conrad** (upper–lower crust), **Mohorovičić** (crust–mantle), **Gutenberg** (mantle–core), Lehmann (outer–inner core).
- **Plate tectonics**: convergent (Himalayas, trenches), divergent (Mid-Atlantic Ridge), transform (San Andreas Fault).
- Volcanoes: Pacific "Ring of Fire"; India's only active volcano: **Barren Island** (Andaman).

### Atmosphere
- Layers: troposphere (weather; about 8 km at poles, 18 km at equator), stratosphere (ozone layer), mesosphere (coldest), thermosphere/ionosphere (radio waves), exosphere.
- Gases: nitrogen 78%, oxygen 21%, argon 0.93%, CO₂ about 0.04%.
- Pressure belts and winds: trade winds, westerlies, polar easterlies. Coriolis force deflects winds right in the north, left in the south.
- Jet streams; **El Niño** (warm Pacific off Peru → weak Indian monsoon), **La Niña** (opposite), **Indian Ocean Dipole**.
- Cyclones: tropical (low pressure, anticlockwise in the north). Names: hurricane (Atlantic), typhoon (west Pacific), willy-willy (Australia).

### Oceans
- Ocean currents: warm (Gulf Stream, Kuroshio), cold (Labrador, Humboldt/Peru, Benguela, California). Fog and fishing grounds where warm and cold meet (Grand Banks).
- Deepest point: **Mariana Trench (Challenger Deep)**.

## 2. India: physical
| Feature | Facts |
|---|---|
| Location | 8°4′N to 37°6′N; 68°7′E to 97°25′E; **Tropic of Cancer** passes through 8 states (Gujarat, Rajasthan, MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, Mizoram) |
| Standard time | 82°30′E (passes near **Mirzapur/Prayagraj, UP**) |

| System | Rivers |
|---|---|
| **Ganga** | Bhagirathi + Alaknanda meet at **Devprayag**; Yamuna joins at **Prayagraj (Sangam)**; left-bank: Ramganga, Gomti, Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi; right-bank: Yamuna, Son |

### Climate and soils
- Monsoon: southwest (June–Sept), retreating (Oct–Nov; rain in Tamil Nadu from the northeast monsoon). Highest rainfall: **Mawsynram** (Meghalaya).
- Soils: alluvial (most extensive; Gangetic plain), black (regur, cotton; Deccan), red, laterite, arid, mountain.
- Natural vegetation: tropical evergreen, deciduous (most widespread), thorn, mountain, mangroves (Sundarbans).

## 3. India: economic geography
- Crops: rice (WB, UP, Punjab), **wheat (UP first)**, **sugarcane (UP first)**, cotton (Gujarat, Maharashtra), tea (Assam), coffee (Karnataka), jute (WB).
- Minerals: iron ore (Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Karnataka), coal (Jharkhand, Odisha, Chhattisgarh), bauxite (Odisha), mica (Andhra, Rajasthan, Jharkhand).
- Multipurpose projects: Bhakra Nangal (Sutlej), Hirakud (Mahanadi), Damodar Valley (1948), Sardar Sarovar (Narmada), **Rihand (Govind Ballabh Pant Sagar, Sonbhadra, UP)**, Tehri (Bhagirathi).

## 4. World geography
- Continents by area: Asia > Africa > North America > South America > Antarctica > Europe > Australia.
- Longest river: Nile; largest by volume: Amazon. Largest desert (hot): Sahara.
- Important straits: **Palk** (India–Sri Lanka), **Malacca** (Malaysia–Indonesia), **Hormuz** (Persian Gulf–Gulf of Oman), Bab-el-Mandeb (Red Sea–Gulf of Aden), Gibraltar, Bering.
- Grasslands: Prairies (N. America), Pampas (Argentina), Steppes (Eurasia), Veld (South Africa), Downs (Australia).

## Exam traps
- India's standard meridian (82°30′E) passes through UP (Mirzapur area).
- Tropic of Cancer does **not** pass through UP or Bihar.
- Ganga and Yamuna meet at Prayagraj; Bhagirathi and Alaknanda meet at Devprayag (Uttarakhand).
- Narmada and Tapi flow west through rift valleys; Godavari is the longest peninsular river.
- UP is first in wheat, sugarcane and milk production.
