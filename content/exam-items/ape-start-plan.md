## Read this first

This exam has been set up for **Assistant Professor (Economics)** and **PGT Economics** exams in all states. The base is the **UGC NET Economics syllabus** because almost every state commission copies it. Anyone opening this site gets, for each of the 10 units:

1. **Long notes**: theory, formulas, diagrams, "who said what" tables and exam traps.
2. **Mind map**: one-screen revision.
3. **Solved practice test**: 15 MCQs in the PYQ pattern, with explanations (timed, with negative marking).
4. **Old PYQ PDFs** where available.

Plus: **Current Economy 2026** (Economic Survey, Union Budget, state budgets) and a **full-length 90-question mock**.

> ⚠️ Practice questions here are written in the PYQ pattern for practice. They are not copies of official papers. Official UGC NET papers and answer keys are on [nta.ac.in](https://nta.ac.in) and [ugcnet.nta.nic.in](https://ugcnet.nta.nic.in).

## 5-day crash plan (revision)

This plan is for **revision** for someone who has studied the subject once. If you are starting from zero, use the 30-day plan below.

| Day | Morning (4 h) | Afternoon (4 h) | Evening (2 h) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Unit 1 Micro: notes + mind map | Unit 2 Macro: notes + mind map | Both practice tests; note every wrong answer |
| **2** | Unit 3 Statistics & Econometrics | Unit 4 Mathematical Economics (do every worked example) | Practice tests 3 and 4; formula sheet |
| **3** | Unit 5 International + Unit 7 Money & Banking | Unit 6 Public Economics | Practice tests 5, 6, 7 |
| **4** | Unit 8 Growth & Development + Unit 9 Environment & Demography | Unit 10 Indian Economy + Current Economy 2026 (all budgets) | Practice tests 8, 9, 10 |
| **5** | **Full mock (90 Qs, timed)** | Revise only your wrong answers + "Exam traps" sections | Formula sheet + "who said what" tables; sleep early |

**GK part (UPESSC, 30 questions):** spend 30 minutes daily on UP-specific GK (UP budget, schemes, geography, history) and national current affairs from the last 6 months.

## How to reach the top 1%

1. **Accuracy over attempts.** With −1 for each wrong answer (3 marks for right), guess only when you can remove two options.
2. **Learn tables, not paragraphs.** Most questions are "matching" or "who gave what". Every unit's notes have such tables; make them your last-day revision.
3. **Numericals are free marks.** Multipliers, elasticities, money multiplier, Gini, index numbers, adjusted R², Harrod–Domar. Practise until each takes under 60 seconds.
4. **Assertion–reason and statement questions** are common in UGC NET and UPESSC. Check every statement; one wrong word makes it false.
5. **Error log.** Keep a notebook of every wrong answer with the correct concept. Revise it every 3 days.
6. **Current data in numbers.** Examiners ask exact figures: Budget size, fiscal deficit, growth projections, FC share. They are in "Current Economy 2026".
7. **Previous years' papers.** Solve the last 5 years of UGC NET Economics (June and December) and your state's last two papers.
8. **Interview (40 marks in UPESSC).** Prepare your PhD/PG research topic, current economic issues (budget, inflation, trade deals), teaching method, NEP 2020.
