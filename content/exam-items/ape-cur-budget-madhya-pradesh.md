## Madhya Pradesh budget 2026-27

- **Size:** ₹4,38,317 crore, presented by Deputy CM and Finance Minister **Jagdish Devda** on 18 Feb 2026.
- **No new taxes**; existing rates unchanged.
- **Rolling three-year budget** framework (2026-27 to 2028-29); outlay planned to reach ₹6.12 lakh crore by 2029-30.
- **GSDP** about ₹18,48,274 crore.
- **Fiscal deficit:** sources differ: 3.87–3.9% of GSDP (PRS, Drishti) vs ₹74,323 crore = 4.45% (ETV Bharat, including central special assistance; within 3% without it).
- About **₹1.27 lakh crore** for women-centred schemes; **₹23,800 crore for Ladli Behna Yojana**.
