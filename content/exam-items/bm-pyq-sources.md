## Previous-year papers: where to get them

Official BPSC papers are not all on one page, so here is where each one is available. **Download them, upload them to this site, and each question will be added with a model answer.**

| Exam | Where | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| All BPSC exams (official) | [bpsc.bihar.gov.in](https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in) → Question papers / Answer keys | papers and answer keys of recent exams |
| CCE Mains Economics optional | [BPSC Pathshala: Economics optional PYQ](https://bpscpathshala.com/bpsc-preparation/bpsc-previous-year-question-papers/bpsc-mains-optional-pyq/bpsc-mains-optional-pyq-economics/) | 68th to 70th (71st also listed) |
| CCE Mains Economics (last 5 years) | [The IAS Hub](https://theiashub.com/upsc/bpsc-mains-economics-question-paper/) | download links |
| BPSC Auditor (Prelims and Mains) | [Adda247](https://www.adda247.com/exams/bihar/bpsc-auditor-previous-year-question-papers/), [Freshersnow](https://www.freshersnow.com/bpsc-auditor-previous-question-papers/) | download links |
| All BPSC papers | [Testbook](https://testbook.com/bpsc-exam/previous-year-papers) | prelims and mains PDFs |

> These are third-party sites (checked by search on 4 Nov 2026); the files themselves were not opened. Prefer the official BPSC site where a paper is available there.

### What repeats in BPSC Economics papers (topic pattern)
Even without the full papers, these themes come again and again in BPSC Economics Mains: **poverty and unemployment in India and Bihar**, **planning and NITI Aayog**, **agriculture and land reforms**, **public finance and the budget**, **inflation and monetary policy**, **national income**, **demand and elasticity**, **market forms**, **international trade and balance of payments**, **economic reforms since 1991**, **Bihar economy** (growth, migration, floods, industry). All are covered in the notes linked from "Economics (Mains): syllabus mapped to full notes".
