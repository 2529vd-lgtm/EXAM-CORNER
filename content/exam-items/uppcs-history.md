## 1. Ancient India

### Prehistory and Harappan (Indus) civilisation (c. 2600–1900 BCE, mature phase)
| Site | River / state | Key finds |
|---|---|---|
| Harappa | Ravi (Pakistan) | first excavated (Daya Ram Sahni, 1921); granaries |
| Mohenjo-daro | Indus (Pakistan) | Great Bath, dancing girl (bronze), priest-king (R.D. Banerji, 1922) |
| Lothal | Bhogavo (Gujarat) | dockyard, rice husk |
| Kalibangan | Ghaggar (Rajasthan) | ploughed field, fire altars |
| Dholavira | Gujarat | water reservoirs, signboard; **UNESCO 2021** |
| Rakhigarhi | Haryana | largest Harappan site in India |
| **Alamgirpur** | Hindon (**Meerut, UP**) | easternmost Harappan site |
| Sanauli | **Baghpat, UP** | burials with chariots (Late Harappan / OCP) |

- Features: planned cities with grid roads, drainage, burnt bricks, seals (unicorn), script undeciphered, no temples, trade with Mesopotamia ("Meluhha").

### Vedic period
- **Rigveda** (c. 1500–1000 BCE): 10 mandalas, 1,028 hymns; Gayatri mantra (3rd mandala); Purusha Sukta (10th mandala: four varnas); Battle of Ten Kings on the Ravi (Parushni).
- Later Vedic (c. 1000–600 BCE): Sama, Yajur, Atharva Vedas; iron (Painted Grey Ware); kingdoms of Kuru and Panchala (western UP); varna system becomes rigid.
- Six schools of philosophy: Nyaya (Gautama), Vaisheshika (Kanada), Sankhya (Kapila), Yoga (Patanjali), Mimamsa (Jaimini), Vedanta (Badarayana).

### Mahajanapadas, Buddhism and Jainism (6th century BCE)
- 16 Mahajanapadas (Anguttara Nikaya). In UP: **Kashi, Kosala, Vatsa (Kaushambi), Kuru, Panchala, Shurasena (Mathura), Malla (Kushinagar)**.
- **Buddha**: born at Lumbini (Nepal), enlightenment at Bodh Gaya, **first sermon at Sarnath (UP)** (Dharmachakra Pravartana), **died at Kushinagar (UP)**. Teachings: four noble truths, eightfold path. Councils: Rajagriha, Vaishali, Pataliputra (Ashoka), Kashmir (Kanishka).
- **Mahavira**: 24th Tirthankara; five vows; Shvetambara and Digambara split.

### Mauryas (321–185 BCE)
- Chandragupta (with Chanakya, *Arthashastra*); Megasthenes' *Indica*.
- **Ashoka**: Kalinga war (261 BCE); dhamma; edicts in Brahmi, Kharosthi, Greek, Aramaic; deciphered by **James Prinsep (1837)**. Pillars in UP: **Sarnath** (lion capital: national emblem), **Allahabad (Prayagraj)** pillar (later Samudragupta's inscription), Meerut and Topra (moved to Delhi by Firoz Tughlaq).

### Post-Maurya and Guptas
- Kushanas: **Kanishka** (78 CE, Shaka era); Mathura and Gandhara art schools (**Mathura school**: red sandstone, indigenous).
- **Guptas** (c. 320–550 CE): Chandragupta I; **Samudragupta** ("Napoleon of India", **Prayag Prashasti** by Harishena); Chandragupta II (Vikramaditya; Fa-Hien visited; Navaratnas: Kalidasa, Aryabhata later). Golden age: Aryabhata (*Aryabhatiya*, zero/place value use), Varahamihira, Kalidasa (*Abhijnanashakuntalam*). Bhitargaon brick temple (**Kanpur**), Dashavatara temple (**Deogarh, Lalitpur**).
- **Harshavardhana** (606–647): capital **Kannauj**; Xuanzang; Banabhatta's *Harshacharita*; Prayag assemblies every five years.

## 2. Medieval India
- Tripartite struggle for **Kannauj** (8th–10th century): Palas, Pratiharas, Rashtrakutas.
- **Delhi Sultanate** (1206–1526): Slave (Qutbuddin Aibak, Iltutmish: iqta, silver tanka; Razia; Balban: "blood and iron"), Khalji (Alauddin: market reforms, standing army), Tughlaq (Muhammad bin Tughlaq: token currency, capital to Daulatabad; Firoz: canals, jizya on Brahmins), Sayyid, Lodi (Sikandar Lodi founded **Agra, 1504**).
- **Jaunpur Sultanate** (Sharqi dynasty, UP): Atala Masjid; "Shiraz of India".
- **Mughals**: Babur (Panipat 1526, Khanwa 1527 vs Rana Sanga, Ghaghra 1529); Humayun (lost to Sher Shah at Chausa 1539 and **Kannauj 1540**); **Sher Shah** (rupiya, Grand Trunk Road, land survey); **Akbar** (Panipat II 1556; mansabdari, zabt, Din-i-Ilahi 1582; **Fatehpur Sikri**, Buland Darwaza; Ibadat Khana); Jahangir (Nur Jahan; Thomas Roe); **Shah Jahan (Taj Mahal, Agra)**; Aurangzeb (jizya re-imposed 1679).
- **Bhakti movement**: Ramananda, **Kabir** (Varanasi), **Tulsidas** (*Ramcharitmanas*, Varanasi), **Surdas** (Agra/Mathura, *Sursagar*), Ravidas (Varanasi), Chaitanya, Guru Nanak. Sufis: Chishti (Moinuddin, Ajmer; Nizamuddin, Delhi), **Salim Chishti (Fatehpur Sikri)**.

## 4. Art and culture (quick)
- Classical dances (8 recognised by Sangeet Natak Akademi): Bharatanatyam, Kathak (**UP**: Lucknow and Banaras gharanas), Kathakali, Kuchipudi, Odissi, Manipuri, Mohiniyattam, Sattriya.
- Music: Hindustani gharanas; **Bismillah Khan** (shehnai, Varanasi; Bharat Ratna 2001).
- Architecture: Nagara (north), Dravida (south), Vesara (mixed).

## Exam traps
- Buddha's first sermon was at **Sarnath**, death at **Kushinagar** (both UP); enlightenment was in Bihar.
- Treaty of Allahabad was 1765, Battle of Buxar 1764.
- Chauri Chaura (1922) is in **Gorakhpur**, Kakori (1925) near **Lucknow**.
- Alamgirpur (Meerut) is the easternmost Harappan site.
- Prayag Prashasti is on the Ashokan pillar at Allahabad, composed by Harishena for Samudragupta.
