### Syllabus (short)
- **General Studies**: Indian history and national movement, Indian and world geography, Indian polity, economy and social development, general science, environment, current affairs, **Uttar Pradesh special** (history, culture, geography, economy, schemes), elementary mathematics and reasoning.
- **General Hindi**: grammar (संधि, समास, पर्यायवाची, विलोम, मुहावरे, वाक्य-शुद्धि), comprehension, Hindi literature basics (authors and works).
- **Mains Hindi and drafting**: official letter, notification, memo, précis, translation, grammar.
- **Hindi essay**: three essays on literature/culture, social, and science/economy themes.

### Preparation tips
1. **Hindi is decisive.** It is 60 marks in prelims and two full papers in mains. Practise grammar daily.
2. **UP special GK**: UP budget, schemes, districts, rivers, festivals, personalities.
3. **ARO**: start Hindi typing practice now (Mangal/Unicode, 25 wpm).
4. Solve the previous RO/ARO papers (2016, 2021, 2023 cycles) from the UPPSC website.
