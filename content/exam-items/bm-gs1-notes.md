### Living culture
- **Paintings**: **Madhubani/Mithila** (GI 2007; Sita Devi, Ganga Devi, Mahasundari Devi, Dulari Devi: Padma awardees), **Manjusha** (Bhagalpur, snake motifs, Bihula–Bishahari story), **Tikuli** (Patna), **Patna Kalam** (Company school, 18th–19th century).
- **Festivals**: **Chhath** (worship of the Sun and Chhathi Maiya; four days, after Diwali), Sama-Chakeva (Mithila), Jitiya, Karma, Shravani Mela (Sultanganj–Deoghar), Sonepur Mela (one of Asia's largest cattle fairs), Rajgir Mahotsav.
- **Folk theatre and music**: **Bidesia** (Bhikhari Thakur, "Shakespeare of Bhojpuri"), Jat-Jatin, Jhijhiya, Sohar, Chaita, Kajri.
- **Languages**: Hindi, Urdu (second official language), **Maithili** (8th Schedule, 2003), Bhojpuri, Magahi, Angika, Bajjika.
- **Literature**: Vidyapati (Maithili poet), Ramdhari Singh **Dinkar** (*Urvashi*, Jnanpith 1972; Rashtrakavi), **Phanishwar Nath Renu** (*Maila Anchal*, regional novel), Nagarjun, Raja Radhika Raman.

## Part 2: Modern history (Bihar focus)

### Revolt of 1857 in Bihar
- **Kunwar Singh** of Jagdishpur (aged about 80) led the revolt in Shahabad; died April 1858; Amar Singh continued.
- **Peer Ali** (Patna) hanged in July 1857; **Waris Ali** (Tirhut) among the first martyrs.
- Danapur cantonment sepoys revolted (25 July 1857).

### Before Gandhi
- **Wahabi movement** (Patna centre; Patna trials 1864–65).
- **Indigo** oppression in Champaran (tinkathia system: 3/20 of land under indigo).
- **Separation of Bihar** from Bengal: demanded by Sachchidanand Sinha, Mahesh Narayan; *The Bihar Herald*, *The Behar Times*; **Bihar and Orissa province formed 1 April 1912**; Orissa separated 1936.
- **Bihar Students' Conference** (1906); Patna College (1863), **Patna University (1917)**.

### Gandhian phase
| Event | Year | Bihar link |
|---|---|---|
| **Champaran Satyagraha** | **1917** | Gandhi's first satyagraha in India; invited by **Rajkumar Shukla**; Rajendra Prasad, Anugrah Narayan Sinha, J.B. Kripalani, Brajkishore Prasad joined; tinkathia abolished by Champaran Agrarian Act 1918 |
| Non-Cooperation | 1920–22 | **Bihar Vidyapeeth** (1921) and **Sadaqat Ashram**, Patna |
| Kisan Sabha | **1929** | **Swami Sahajanand Saraswati** formed the Bihar Provincial Kisan Sabha; All India Kisan Sabha 1936 |
| Civil Disobedience | 1930 | salt satyagraha at Bihpur, Begusarai; chaukidari tax boycott |
| Quit India | **1942** | **11 August 1942: seven students shot** while hoisting the flag at Patna Secretariat (Martyrs' Memorial); parallel governments; **Jayaprakash Narayan** escaped Hazaribagh jail (Nov 1942) |

- Other leaders: **Dr Rajendra Prasad** (first President), Anugrah Narayan Sinha, Shri Krishna Sinha (first CM), Maulana Mazharul Haque, Jagjivan Ram.
- Post-independence: **JP movement** (Sampoorna Kranti, **5 June 1974**, Gandhi Maidan); Bihar Land Reforms Act 1950 (first zamindari abolition law challenged in courts: Kameshwar Singh case).
- Jharkhand separated **15 November 2000**.

### Modern India topics BPSC asks often
Tribal movements (Santhal Hul 1855–56: Sidhu and Kanhu; Birsa Munda's Ulgulan 1899–1900), peasant movements, press and education, social reform, role of women, revolutionary movements (Batukeshwar Dutt, Yogendra Shukla), INA.

## Part 4: Statistical analysis, graphs and diagrams
This part is pure scoring. Typical questions: mean, median, mode, percentage, ratio, average, compound growth, reading a table or chart.

### Quick formulas
- Percentage change = (new − old)/old × 100.
- **CAGR** = (final/initial)^(1/n) − 1.
- Weighted mean = Σwx/Σw.
- Median (grouped) = L + [(N/2 − cf)/f] × h.
- Mode (grouped) = L + [(f₁ − f₀)/(2f₁ − f₀ − f₂)] × h.
- Standard deviation = √[Σ(x − x̄)²/n].

### Worked examples
**1.** Per-capita GSDP rose from ₹46,412 (2020-21) to ₹76,490 (2024-25). Percentage increase?
(76,490 − 46,412)/46,412 × 100 = 30,078/46,412 × 100 ≈ **64.8%**. CAGR over 4 years = (76,490/46,412)^(1/4) − 1 ≈ 1.648^0.25 − 1 ≈ **13.3%**.

**2.** Marks: 45, 52, 61, 52, 70. Mean = 280/5 = **56**; median = **52**; mode = **52**.

**3.** A pie chart has Agriculture 72°, Industry 54°, Services 234°. Share of services = 234/360 = **65%**.

**4.** Ratio of A:B = 3:5 and B:C = 2:3. A:B:C = 6:10:15.
