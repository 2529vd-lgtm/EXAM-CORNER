# Statistics & Econometrics

## Averages
- AM ≥ GM ≥ HM
- GM² = AM × HM
- Mode = 3Md − 2Mean

## Distributions
- Binomial: np, npq
- Poisson: mean = variance
- Normal: 68–95–99.7

## Testing
- Type I = α
- Power = 1 − β
- z, t, χ², F tests

## Regression
- r = √(byx·bxy)
- R², adjusted R²
- OLS = BLUE (Gauss–Markov)

## Problems
- Multicollinearity: VIF
- Heteroscedasticity: White, BP, GQ
- Autocorrelation: DW ≈ 2(1 − ρ)
- Endogeneity: IV/2SLS

## Time series
- Unit root: ADF
- Cointegration: Engle–Granger
- Spurious: R² > DW

## Index numbers
- Laspeyres: base qty
- Paasche: current qty
- Fisher: ideal
