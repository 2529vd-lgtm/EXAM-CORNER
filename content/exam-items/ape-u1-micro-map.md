# Micro Economics

## Demand
- Cardinal: MUx/Px = MUy/Py
- Ordinal: MRS = Px/Py
- Price effect = SE + IE
- Giffen: inferior, IE > SE
- Revealed preference: Samuelson
- Elasticity: MR = P(1 − 1/e)

## Production & cost
- Law of variable proportions: stage II
- Cobb–Douglas σ = 1
- MC cuts AVC, AC at minimum
- LAC = envelope

## Markets
- Perfect competition: P = MC
- Monopoly: MR = MC < P, DWL
- Monopolistic competition: excess capacity
- Oligopoly: Cournot, Bertrand, Stackelberg, Sweezy

## Game theory
- Nash equilibrium
- Prisoner's dilemma
- Maximin = minimax (saddle point)

## Distribution
- MRP = factor price
- Ricardian rent, quasi-rent
- Knight: uncertainty profit
- Schumpeter: innovation

## Welfare
- Pareto conditions
- Two welfare theorems
- Kaldor–Hicks, Scitovsky
- Arrow impossibility
- Second best

## Information
- Adverse selection: Akerlof
- Signalling: Spence
- Moral hazard
