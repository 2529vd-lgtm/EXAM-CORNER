## State-wise Assistant Professor & PGT Economics exams

North India first, then all other states and central exams. Data collected from news and coaching sites on **4 November 2026**; numbers marked "reported" conflict between sources. **Always confirm on the official website** before applying.

**Level:** AP = Assistant Professor (college/university, needs NET/SET/PhD) · PGT = school lecturer for classes 11–12 (needs PG + B.Ed. in most states).
