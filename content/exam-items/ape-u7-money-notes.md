## 1. Functions and types of money
- Functions: medium of exchange, unit of account, store of value, standard of deferred payment.
- **Gresham's law**: bad money drives good money out of circulation.
- Fiat money, near money (time deposits, bonds), **CBDC**: RBI's e-rupee (pilot from Dec 2022).

## 2. Demand for money

| Theory | Author | Key equation / idea |
|---|---|---|
| **Quantity theory (transactions)** | Irving **Fisher** (1911) | **MV = PT**; V and T constant → P ∝ M |
| **Cash balance (Cambridge)** | Marshall, Pigou, Robertson, Keynes (early) | **M = kPY**; k = 1/V; money as store of value |
| **Liquidity preference** | **Keynes** (1936) | transactions + precautionary (depend on Y) + **speculative** (depends inversely on r); liquidity trap at very low r |
| Inventory-theoretic | **Baumol (1952), Tobin (1956)** | transactions demand ∝ √(Y·b/2r): square-root rule; economies of scale in money holding |
| Portfolio / risk aversion | **Tobin** (1958) | people hold a mix of money and bonds to reduce risk |
| **Modern quantity theory** | **Friedman** (1956) | money is an asset; demand is a stable function of permanent income, returns on bonds, equities, expected inflation |
| Patinkin | Don Patinkin | real balance effect integrates money and value theory |

## 3. Money supply in India
| Measure | Components |
|---|---|
| **M0** (reserve money / high-powered money) | currency in circulation + bankers' deposits with RBI + other deposits with RBI |
| **M1** (narrow money) | currency with public + demand deposits with banks + other deposits with RBI |
| M2 | M1 + savings deposits with post office savings banks |
| **M3** (broad money) | M1 + **time deposits** with banks |
| M4 | M3 + all deposits with post office savings (excluding NSC) |

- Most liquid: M1. Most used for broad money: **M3** ("aggregate monetary resources").
- **Money multiplier** m = M/H = (1 + c)/(c + r), where c = currency–deposit ratio, r = reserve ratio. Simple deposit multiplier = 1/r.
- Sources of reserve money: RBI's net credit to government, to banks, to the commercial sector, net foreign exchange assets, government currency liabilities to the public.

## 4. Reserve Bank of India
- Established **1 April 1935** (Hilton Young Commission); nationalised **1949**.
- **Monetary Policy Committee** (MPC): from 2016 under the amended RBI Act (Urjit Patel committee recommendation). Six members (3 RBI incl. Governor, 3 external); Governor has a casting vote.
- **Flexible inflation targeting**: **CPI inflation 4% ± 2%** (band 2–6%), set for 2016–21, retained for 2021–26 and again for 2026–31.
- Failure: inflation outside the band for **three consecutive quarters** → RBI must report to the government.

### Policy instruments
| Quantitative | Qualitative |
|---|---|
| Repo rate (policy rate), reverse repo, **Standing Deposit Facility (SDF)** (floor of LAF corridor since 2022), **MSF** (ceiling) | selective credit controls, margin requirements |
| CRR (cash with RBI), SLR (liquid assets with bank) | moral suasion, direct action |
| Open market operations, OMO, variable rate repo/reverse repo | credit rationing |
| Bank rate (same as MSF rate) | |

- LAF corridor: SDF = repo − 25 bps; MSF = repo + 25 bps.
- **Transmission**: external benchmark lending rates (EBLR) since **October 2019** for retail and MSME floating loans.

## 5. Commercial banking
- Credit creation: deposit multiplier 1/r (in the simple model).
- Bank nationalisation: **14 banks (1969)**, **6 banks (1980)**. Lead Bank Scheme 1969. RRBs **1975** (Narasimham working group). NABARD **1982**. SIDBI 1990. EXIM Bank 1982.
- **Narasimham Committee I (1991)** and **II (1998)**: lower CRR/SLR, deregulate interest rates, prudential norms, capital adequacy, fewer but stronger banks.
- **Basel norms**: Basel I (1988), II (2004: three pillars: minimum capital, supervisory review, market discipline), **III** (2010: capital conservation buffer, leverage ratio, LCR, NSFR). India's minimum CRAR 9% (higher than Basel's 8%).
- NPAs: an asset is NPA if interest/instalment is overdue for **90 days**. Resolution: SARFAESI Act 2002, **IBC 2016**, NARCL ("bad bank", 2021).
- **Bank mergers 2019–20**: 27 PSBs reduced to 12.
- Payments banks and small finance banks (Nachiket Mor committee, 2014 licences).
- Financial inclusion: **PMJDY (Aug 2014)**, UPI (NPCI, 2016), Aadhaar-enabled payments, the JAM trinity.

## 6. Financial markets
- **Money market** (short term, < 1 year): call money, T-bills (91, 182, 364 days), commercial paper, certificates of deposit, repo, TREPS.
- **Capital market**: primary and secondary; SEBI (statutory 1992).
- **Theories**: efficient market hypothesis (Fama), Modigliani–Miller theorem (capital structure irrelevance), CAPM (Sharpe), term structure of interest rates (expectations, liquidity premium, segmented markets).
- **Fisher effect**: nominal interest rate = real rate + expected inflation.
- **Taylor rule**: policy rate responds to inflation gap and output gap.

## 7. Inflation and monetary policy debates
- Rules vs discretion (**Kydland & Prescott, time inconsistency**). Central bank independence.
- **Monetarist k% rule** (Friedman).
- **Tinbergen**: number of instruments ≥ number of targets.
- Inflation measures in India: CPI (combined) for targeting; WPI for producer prices; GDP deflator broadest.

## Exam traps
- M3 includes time deposits; M1 does not.
- In the money multiplier, a rise in the currency–deposit ratio **reduces** the multiplier.
- Bank rate in India now equals the MSF rate.
- Keynes' speculative demand varies inversely with the interest rate; transactions demand varies with income.
- Baumol–Tobin: transactions demand rises with the **square root** of income (income elasticity = 0.5).
