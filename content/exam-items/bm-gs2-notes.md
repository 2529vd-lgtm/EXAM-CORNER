> **GS Paper II (300 marks).** Three parts: Indian polity, Indian economy and geography of India (with Bihar), and the role of science and technology in the development of India and Bihar.

## Part 1: Indian polity (with Bihar)

### Constitution basics to quote
- Preamble, Fundamental Rights (Part III), DPSP (Part IV), Fundamental Duties (Part IVA, 42nd Amendment 1976), basic structure (**Kesavananda Bharati, 1973**).
- **Federalism**: 7th Schedule lists; Article 356; inter-state council (Article 263); Finance Commission (Article 280); GST Council (Article 279A).
- **Local government**: **73rd and 74th Amendments (1992)**; Part IX and IXA; 11th and 12th Schedules.

### State government in Bihar
- **Bicameral legislature**: Vidhan Sabha (243 seats) and Vidhan Parishad (75 seats).
- Bihar has **40 Lok Sabha** and **16 Rajya Sabha** seats.
- Governor (Article 153), Chief Minister and Council of Ministers (Article 163–164), High Court at **Patna (1916)**.
- **Bihar Panchayat Raj Act 2006**: **50% reservation for women** in panchayats, the first state to do so; 20% for EBCs.
- **Bihar Right to Public Services Act 2011**: time-bound delivery of services; **Bihar Lok Shikayat Nivaran Adhikar Adhiniyam 2015** (Right to Public Grievance Redressal).
- **Bihar Special Courts Act 2009** (confiscation of property of corrupt officials).
- **Prohibition and Excise Act 2016**.
- **Caste survey 2023** and the reservation increase to **65%** (struck down by **Patna High Court, June 2024**; Supreme Court did not stay the order).

### Frequently asked themes
Governor's discretion, anti-defection (10th Schedule), electoral reforms, judicial activism and PIL, women's reservation (106th Amendment, 2023: one-third seats in Lok Sabha and assemblies after the next delimitation), cooperative federalism, panchayat finances.

### Geography of Bihar
| Topic | Facts |
|---|---|
| Area and position | 94,163 sq km; landlocked; borders Nepal (north), West Bengal, Jharkhand, UP |
| Physiography | **Shivalik/Someshwar hills** (northwest, West Champaran), **Gangetic plain** (north and south), **southern plateau fringe** (Kaimur, Rajgir hills) |
| Rivers north of Ganga | **Ghaghara, Gandak, Burhi Gandak, Bagmati, Kamla, Kosi, Mahananda** (Himalayan, perennial, flood-prone) |
| Rivers south of Ganga | **Son** (longest southern tributary), Punpun, Phalgu, Karmanasa, Kiul (seasonal) |
| **Kosi** | "Sorrow of Bihar"; shifts course westward; huge sediment load; **Kosi embankment breach at Kusaha (2008)** |
| Floods | about **73% of Bihar's area is flood-prone**; north Bihar most affected |
| Drought | south and southwest Bihar (Gaya, Aurangabad, Nawada, Jamui) |
| Climate | humid subtropical; most rain from the southwest monsoon (about 1,000–1,200 mm average) |
| Soils | alluvial (khadar, bangar), tal and diara lands, red soils in the south |
| Forests | about 7–8% of area (low); **Valmiki Tiger Reserve** (West Champaran, Bihar's only tiger reserve); **Vikramshila Gangetic Dolphin Sanctuary** (Sultanganj–Kahalgaon) |
| Wetlands (Ramsar) | **Kabartal** (Begusarai, first Ramsar site of Bihar), Nagi and Nakti (Jamui), and more added since 2024 |
| Minerals | limited after 2000: pyrite, limestone (Rohtas), mica (Nawada), quartz |
| Administration | 38 districts, 9 divisions |

## Part 3: Science and technology in development (India and Bihar)
- **Space**: Chandrayaan-3 (south pole landing, 23 Aug 2023: National Space Day), Aditya-L1 (2023), Gaganyaan programme, NavIC.
- **Digital public infrastructure**: Aadhaar, UPI, DigiLocker, ONDC, Bhashini; Bihar's e-governance (RTPS counters, e-Kalyan for scholarships).
- **Energy**: India's 2070 net zero; 500 GW non-fossil target by 2030; National Green Hydrogen Mission (2023); Bihar's floating solar projects on water bodies and solar parks under state renewable energy policy.
- **Agriculture and biotech**: high-yield seeds, Bihar Agricultural University (Sabour), makhana research (ICAR–NRC for Makhana, Darbhanga).
- **Health**: AIIMS Patna, telemedicine (e-Sanjeevani), vaccines.
- **Semiconductors and AI**: India Semiconductor Mission (2.0 in Budget 2026-27), IndiaAI Mission (2024).
