> **Indian Polity: basic to advanced.** About 15–20 questions. Learn articles, amendments and bodies in tables; read the traps last.

## 1. Making of the Constitution
- **Constituent Assembly**: formed under the Cabinet Mission Plan (1946); first meeting **9 Dec 1946** (Sachchidananda Sinha, temporary chairman); **Dr Rajendra Prasad** permanent president; **B.R. Ambedkar** chairman of the Drafting Committee; Objectives Resolution by Nehru (13 Dec 1946).
- Adopted **26 Nov 1949** (Constitution Day); in force **26 Jan 1950**. Took 2 years, 11 months, 18 days.
- Original: 395 articles, 22 parts, 8 schedules. Now: 12 schedules, about 470 articles.

## 2. Preamble and features
- Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic. "Socialist", "secular" and "integrity" were added by the **42nd Amendment (1976)**.
- Preamble is part of the Constitution (**Kesavananda Bharati, 1973**); it can be amended but not its basic structure.

## 3. Fundamental Rights (Part III, Articles 12–35)
| Right | Articles |
|---|---|
| Equality | 14–18 (Art. 17: untouchability abolished) |
| Freedom | 19–22 (Art. 19: six freedoms; Art. 21: life and personal liberty; **21A: education 6–14 years**, 86th Amendment 2002) |
| Against exploitation | 23–24 |
| Religion | 25–28 |
| Cultural and educational | 29–30 |
| **Constitutional remedies** | **32** ("heart and soul", Ambedkar) |

- **Right to property** removed from FRs by the **44th Amendment (1978)**; now a legal right under **Article 300A**.
- Writs: habeas corpus, mandamus, prohibition, certiorari, quo warranto. Supreme Court (Art. 32), High Courts (Art. 226, wider).
- Articles 20 and 21 cannot be suspended even during an emergency (44th Amendment).

## 4. DPSP and Fundamental Duties
- **DPSP** (Part IV, Articles 36–51): not justiciable. Art. 40 (panchayats), Art. 44 (uniform civil code), Art. 45 (early childhood care), Art. 48A (environment), Art. 50 (separation of judiciary from executive).
- **Fundamental Duties** (Part IVA, **Article 51A**): added by the 42nd Amendment on the **Swaran Singh Committee**'s advice; now **11** (the 11th, education of children 6–14, added by the 86th Amendment).

## 5. Union government
| Body | Key articles and facts |

| PM and Council of Ministers | Art. 74–75; size limited to 15% of Lok Sabha (91st Amendment, 2003) |

| CAG | Art. 148 |
| Attorney General | Art. 76 |

## 6. State government (with UP)
- Governor (Art. 153–162), CM (Art. 163–164), High Court (Art. 214).
- **UP**: bicameral; **Vidhan Sabha 403**, **Vidhan Parishad 100**; **80 Lok Sabha** and **31 Rajya Sabha** seats (the most). **Allahabad High Court** (1866; bench at Lucknow).

## 7. Federalism and emergencies
- Seventh Schedule: Union List, State List, Concurrent List (originally 97, 66, 47 entries).
- **Article 356** (President's Rule), **Article 352** (national emergency: war, external aggression, armed rebellion; 44th Amendment replaced "internal disturbance"), **Article 360** (financial emergency: never used).
- Inter-State Council (Art. 263); Finance Commission (Art. 280); GST Council (Art. 279A).

## 8. Local government
- **73rd Amendment (1992)**: panchayats, Part IX, 11th Schedule (29 subjects); in force 24 April 1993 (National Panchayati Raj Day).
- **74th Amendment (1992)**: municipalities, Part IXA, 12th Schedule (18 subjects).
- Committees: Balwant Rai Mehta (1957, three tiers), Ashok Mehta (1977, two tiers), G.V.K. Rao, L.M. Singhvi.

## Exam traps
- Right to property is a **constitutional (Art. 300A)**, not a fundamental, right.
- Money Bill: Art. 110; joint sitting: Art. 108; no joint sitting for Money Bills.
- Fundamental Duties: 11, in Art. 51A, added by the 42nd Amendment.
- The President is elected by **elected** members only; nominated members don't vote.
- UP has 80 Lok Sabha seats, 31 Rajya Sabha seats, 403 MLAs, 100 MLCs.
