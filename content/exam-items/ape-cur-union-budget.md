## Union Budget 2026-27: highlights

- **Presented:** 1 February 2026 by Finance Minister **Nirmala Sitharaman**.
- **Framework:** three "Kartavyas" (duties): accelerate economic growth, fulfil citizens' aspirations, ensure inclusive access to resources.

### Key numbers
| Item | 2026-27 (BE) |
|---|---|
| **Total expenditure** | about **₹53.47 lakh crore** |
| **Capital expenditure** | about **₹12.2 lakh crore** (highest ever); effective capex about ₹17.15 lakh crore |
| **Fiscal deficit** | **4.3% of GDP** (from 4.4% RE in 2025-26) |
| **Debt-to-GDP** | about **55.6%**; target about 50% by 2030 |
| Net tax receipts | about ₹28.7 lakh crore |
| Interest payments | over ₹14 lakh crore (about one-fifth of spending) |
| Capex as % of GDP | about 3.1% (RBI Bulletin) |

### Major announcements
- **16th Finance Commission** (2026–31) recommendations accepted: states' vertical share kept at **41%**.
- **Income-tax Act, 2025** replaces the Income-tax Act, 1961 (simpler language and forms). No change in income tax rates in this budget.
- Seven new **high-speed rail corridors** (UP benefits from Delhi–Varanasi and Varanasi–Siliguri).
- **India Semiconductor Mission 2.0**.

### Revise with Budget basics
- Article **112**: Annual Financial Statement. Article 110: Money Bill. Article 113: demands for grants. Article 114: Appropriation Bill.
- Budget day moved to **1 February** in 2017; Railway budget merged with the Union Budget in 2017.
