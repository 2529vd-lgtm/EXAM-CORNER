- **Planning Commission**: 15 March 1950 (cabinet resolution, non-statutory). **National Development Council** 1952.
- **NITI Aayog**: 1 January 2015 (cabinet resolution). Think tank; "cooperative federalism"; 15-year vision, 7-year strategy, 3-year action agenda. Chairman: PM. Governing Council includes all CMs.
- Bombay Plan (1944), Gandhian Plan (S.N. Agarwal), People's Plan (M.N. Roy), Sarvodaya Plan (J.P. Narayan).

## 2. Economic reforms (1991)
- **BoP crisis**: forex reserves enough for about 2–3 weeks of imports; gold pledged.
- **New Industrial Policy, 24 July 1991**: licensing abolished except a few industries, MRTP limits removed, PSU reservation reduced, FDI up to 51% automatic in priority industries.
- **LPG**: liberalisation, privatisation, globalisation. Rupee devalued (July 1991); LERMS (1992); current account convertibility (August 1994).
- Committees: **Narasimham** (banking, 1991 and 1998), **Chelliah** (tax reforms, 1991), **Malhotra** (insurance, 1994), **Rangarajan** (disinvestment, 1993), **Tarapore** (capital account convertibility 1997, 2006), **Kelkar** (tax 2002; FRBM).

## 3. Agriculture
- Share in GVA about 16–18%; employs about 45% of workers (PLFS).
- **Green Revolution** (1966–67): HYV seeds (Norman Borlaug, M.S. Swaminathan), mainly wheat, Punjab–Haryana–western UP.
- Land reforms: zamindari abolition, tenancy reform, land ceilings, consolidation. **Bhoodan** (Vinoba Bhave, 1951).
- **MSP**: announced by Government on **CACP** recommendations (CACP 1965) for 22 mandated crops + FRP for sugarcane. Since 2018-19, MSP at least **1.5 times A2 + FL** cost.
- Schemes: **PM-KISAN** (Feb 2019; ₹6,000/yr in three instalments), PMFBY (crop insurance, 2016), Soil Health Card (2015), e-NAM (2016), PM-KUSUM, Agriculture Infrastructure Fund, **PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana** (Budget 2025-26: 100 low-productivity districts).
- Food security: **NFSA 2013** (75% rural, 50% urban; 5 kg/person/month); PMGKAY free grains extended for five years from January 2024.

## 4. Industry and services
- Industrial policies: 1948, **1956** (Schedules A, B, C), 1977, 1980, **1991**.
- **MSME** definition (2020, revised in Budget 2025-26): investment and turnover limits; micro, small, medium.
- **Make in India** (Sept 2014), **PLI schemes** (2020; 14 sectors), Startup India (2016), National Manufacturing Policy (2011; 25% share target).
- Core sector: eight core industries (coal, crude oil, natural gas, refinery products, fertilisers, steel, cement, electricity), weight about 40% in IIP; refinery products have the highest weight.
- Services: more than half of GVA. IT-BPM, tourism, financial services.
- Disinvestment: DIPAM; strategic sale of Air India to Tatas (Jan 2022).

## 5. Poverty, unemployment and inequality
- Poverty lines: see Unit 8 (Tendulkar, Rangarajan, NITI MPI).
- Employment data: **PLFS** (NSO, since 2017-18; now monthly and quarterly). Measures: usual status (UPS + SS), current weekly status (CWS).
- Schemes: **MGNREGA** (2005; 100 days of wage work; rights-based) was **replaced by the VB-G RAM G Act** (Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025; Presidential assent Dec 2025) from **1 July 2026**: guarantee raised to **125 days**, average notified wage ₹327.4/day (reported), DAY-NRLM (2011, Aajeevika), PMEGP (2008), PM SVANidhi (2020), Skill India, **PM Vishwakarma** (2023).
- Labour codes: four codes (wages, industrial relations, social security, occupational safety); **came into force 21 November 2025**.

## 6. Inflation in India
- **CPI** (NSO) for inflation targeting; **WPI** (Office of Economic Adviser, DPIIT).
- Food inflation is volatile; supply-side and monsoon-driven.
- Recent trend: CPI inflation fell to very low levels in 2025 (Economic Survey 2025-26: average 1.7% in April–December 2025), then rose in 2026 (RBI raised the repo rate in October 2026).

## 7. Fiscal system
- Union Budget: Article **112** (Annual Financial Statement). Money bill: Article 110. Finance Commission: Article 280. GST Council: Article 279A.
- Tax-to-GDP (Centre + states) about 18%.
- See Unit 6 for deficits, FRBM, FC shares and GST.

## 8. External sector
- **FEMA 1999** replaced FERA 1973.
- Rupee: managed float; **current account** fully convertible, capital account partially.
- Trade: India's largest trading partners: USA, China, UAE. Major exports: petroleum products, engineering goods, electronics (smartphones), gems and jewellery, pharma.
- FTAs: India–UAE CEPA (2022), India–Australia ECTA (2022), **India–EFTA TEPA** (signed 2024; in force 1 Oct 2025), **India–UK CETA** (signed July 2025), **India–EU FTA** (negotiations concluded **27 Jan 2026** at the 16th India–EU Summit after talks since 2007; duty-free access for over 99% of Indian exports by value; formal signing and ratification pending as reported).
- Remittances: India is the world's largest recipient.
- Forex reserves: check RBI's weekly statistical supplement (around $650–700 bn range in 2025–26).

## Exam traps
- NITI Aayog replaced the Planning Commission on 1 Jan 2015; neither is constitutional or statutory.
- The 2nd Plan used the Mahalanobis model, not Harrod–Domar.
- Eighth Plan was the first to adopt indicative planning after liberalisation.
- MSP is announced by the Government, recommended by CACP.
- WPI is compiled by the Office of Economic Adviser (DPIIT), not NSO.
