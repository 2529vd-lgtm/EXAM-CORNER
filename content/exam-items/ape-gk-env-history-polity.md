## Unit 4: People and environment
### Human–environment interaction
- Pollution: **air** (PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, SO₂, ozone; AQI has 6 categories from Good to Severe), **water** (BOD: higher BOD = more polluted), **soil**, **noise** (decibels), e-waste, plastic.
- Effects on health: respiratory disease, Minamata (mercury), Itai-itai (cadmium), blue baby syndrome (nitrates), fluorosis (fluoride), arsenicosis.
- Natural hazards: earthquake, flood, cyclone, drought, landslide, heat wave. **NDMA** (Disaster Management Act 2005; PM is chair). **Sendai Framework** (2015–2030).

### Energy
- Renewable: solar, wind, hydro, biomass, geothermal, tidal. Non-renewable: coal, oil, gas, nuclear (uranium).
- India: target of **500 GW non-fossil capacity by 2030**; **International Solar Alliance** (2015, HQ Gurugram); National Green Hydrogen Mission (2023); PM Surya Ghar (rooftop solar, 2024).

- Indian laws: Wildlife Protection 1972, Water 1974, Forest Conservation 1980, Air 1981, Environment Protection 1986, Biodiversity 2002, **National Green Tribunal Act 2010**.
- Mission LiFE (Lifestyle for Environment, 2022). National Action Plan on Climate Change (2008; 8 missions).
