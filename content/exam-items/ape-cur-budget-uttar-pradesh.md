## Uttar Pradesh budget 2026-27

- **Size:** ₹9,12,696.35 crore (about ₹9.12–9.13 lakh crore), about **12.2% more** than last year. Largest state budget in India.
- **Revenue expenditure** ₹6,64,470.55 crore; **capital expenditure** ₹2,48,225.80 crore (about 19.5% of the budget).
- **Fiscal deficit:** 3% of GSDP (about ₹1.18 lakh crore).
- **Committed expenditure** (salaries, pensions, interest): ₹3,53,743 crore, 49% of revenue receipts (PRS).
- **No new taxes.** Focus: jobs, infrastructure, health.
- New **Uttar Pradesh Employment Mission**: target of employment for 10 lakh youth.
- About ₹34,000 crore for roads and bridges (reported).
- **Supplementary budget:** ₹59,019.54 crore in the monsoon session (August 2026).
- Useful for UPESSC GK (30 questions).
