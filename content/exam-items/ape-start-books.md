## Practice books
- UGC NET Economics previous-years papers (any recent edition: Trueman's, Arihant, Upkar), solved and unit-wise.
- Your target state's previous papers (UPESSC/UPHESC, MPPSC, RPSC, HPSC).

## Free official sources (read these, they are exam favourites)
- Economic Survey: [indiabudget.gov.in/economicsurvey](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/economicsurvey)
- Union Budget: [indiabudget.gov.in](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in)
- RBI Monetary Policy Statements and Annual Report: [rbi.org.in](https://www.rbi.org.in)
- State budget analyses (one page per state): [prsindia.org/budgets/states](https://prsindia.org/budgets/states)
- MoSPI (GDP, CPI, PLFS): [mospi.gov.in](https://www.mospi.gov.in)
- NITI Aayog reports (MPI, SDG Index): [niti.gov.in](https://www.niti.gov.in)
