## Economic Survey 2025-26: highlights

- **Tabled:** 29 January 2026, in Parliament (prepared under the Chief Economic Adviser, Ministry of Finance).
- **FY26 growth (First Advance Estimates):** real **GDP 7.4%**, real **GVA 7.3%**.
- **FY27 projection:** real GDP growth of **6.8–7.2%**.
- **Potential growth** of the Indian economy: about **7%**.
- **Private consumption** grew about 7.0% in FY26 and reached **61.5% of GDP**, the highest share since 2012.
- **Inflation:** CPI inflation averaged about **1.7% in April–December 2025**, very low because of falling food prices.
- **Trade:** the Survey links the India–EU FTA (concluded January 2026) to stronger manufacturing competitiveness.
- **Risks:** fragile global stability, trade tensions, downside risks to world output.
- **Theme:** steady growth amid global uncertainty; the path to *Viksit Bharat @2047*.

### Why it matters for the exam
- Expect direct questions: "FY27 growth projected by Economic Survey 2025-26 is…" (6.8–7.2%), "private consumption share of GDP…" (61.5%), "date of tabling…".
- Use these figures in the interview and in descriptive answers.

### Practice
<details><summary>Q1. The Economic Survey 2025-26 projected India's FY27 real GDP growth at?</summary>

**6.8–7.2%.** FY26 (advance estimate) was 7.4%.
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. Who presents the Economic Survey and who prepares it?</summary>

It is presented by the **Union Finance Minister** a day or so before the Budget, and prepared by the **Economic Division of the Department of Economic Affairs under the Chief Economic Adviser**. It is not a constitutional requirement.
</details>
