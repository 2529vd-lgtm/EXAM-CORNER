### Scoring strategy
- **Negative marking**: wrong answer costs one-third. Attempt when you can eliminate two options.
- UPPCS GS Paper I has many **UP-specific** and **static** questions; current affairs is about 15–20 questions.
- **CSAT is qualifying, but many fail it.** Do not skip it.
- Previous papers: [uppsc.up.nic.in](https://uppsc.up.nic.in) (question papers and answer keys).
