### Key laws and facts
- Newton's laws: inertia; F = ma; action–reaction (rocket propulsion).
- Speed of sound: air about 332 m/s (faster in solids); light: 3 × 10⁸ m/s.
- Sound: audible 20 Hz–20 kHz; ultrasound above 20 kHz (SONAR, bats); infrasound below 20 Hz.
- Light: reflection, refraction (twinkling of stars, mirage), total internal reflection (diamond sparkle, optical fibre), dispersion (rainbow), scattering (**blue sky**, red sunset: Rayleigh). Raman effect (C.V. Raman, **28 Feb 1928**: National Science Day).
- Lenses: myopia (near-sighted) → **concave** lens; hypermetropia → **convex** lens.
- Electricity: Ohm's law V = IR; household supply in India 220 V, 50 Hz; fuse works on the heating effect.
- Archimedes' principle (buoyancy); Bernoulli's principle (aeroplane lift).

## 2. Chemistry
- States of matter; atomic structure: electron (J.J. Thomson), proton (Rutherford/Goldstein), neutron (Chadwick, 1932).
- pH: below 7 acidic, 7 neutral, above 7 basic. Human blood pH about 7.4. Stomach: HCl.
| Common name | Chemical |
|---|---|
| Baking soda | sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO₃) |
| Washing soda | sodium carbonate (Na₂CO₃·10H₂O) |
| Bleaching powder | calcium oxychloride (CaOCl₂) |
| Plaster of Paris | CaSO₄·½H₂O |
| Laughing gas | nitrous oxide (N₂O) |
| Dry ice | solid CO₂ |
| Quicklime | calcium oxide (CaO) |
- Metals: mercury is liquid at room temperature; tungsten has the highest melting point (bulb filament); gold and platinum are noble metals; **brass = Cu + Zn**, **bronze = Cu + Sn**, steel = Fe + C.
- Gases: LPG is mainly butane and propane (with ethyl mercaptan for smell); CNG is mainly methane; biogas mainly methane.

## 3. Biology
### Cell and genetics
- Cell theory (Schleiden and Schwann). Mitochondria: powerhouse; ribosomes: protein synthesis; lysosomes: "suicide bags".
- DNA double helix: Watson and Crick (1953). Human chromosomes: 46 (23 pairs); sex determined by the father's chromosome (X or Y).
- Blood groups (Landsteiner): O universal donor, AB universal recipient.

## 4. Environment and ecology
- Ecosystem: producers → consumers → decomposers; 10% energy rule (Lindeman).
- Biodiversity hotspots in India: **Himalaya, Indo-Burma, Western Ghats–Sri Lanka, Sundaland (Nicobar)**.
- Protected areas: national parks, wildlife sanctuaries, biosphere reserves (India has 18; 12 in UNESCO's network).
- Acts: Wildlife Protection Act 1972, Water Act 1974, Forest Conservation Act 1980 (amended 2023), Air Act 1981, **Environment Protection Act 1986** (after Bhopal), Biological Diversity Act 2002.
- Climate: Kyoto 1997, Paris 2015, India net zero by **2070**; ozone: Montreal Protocol 1987.
- Greenhouse gases: CO₂, methane, nitrous oxide, water vapour, CFCs.

## Exam traps
- Sky looks blue because of **scattering**, not reflection.
- Myopia is corrected by a **concave** lens.
- Vitamin C deficiency causes scurvy; vitamin D deficiency causes rickets.
- Malaria is caused by a protozoan (Plasmodium), spread by female Anopheles.
- Brass is copper + zinc; bronze is copper + tin.
