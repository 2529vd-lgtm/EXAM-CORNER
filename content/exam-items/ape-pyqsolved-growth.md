force are growing, respectively, at 8% and 3% per annum. If the rate of growth of output is

**Q8.** Harrod's Growth model is given as under:

St = It  
where St = Savings, Yt = Income, It = Investment, t = time  
In this model for economic growth, the condition for economic growth is *[December 2018 · Q.137]*

(1) β/(β − α) > 1  
(2) β/(β − α) < 0
