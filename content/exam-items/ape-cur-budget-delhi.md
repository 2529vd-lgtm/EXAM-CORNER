## Delhi budget 2026-27

- **Size:** ₹1,03,700 crore, presented by **CM Rekha Gupta** on 24 Mar 2026 (her second budget). PRS puts total expenditure excluding debt repayment at ₹99,446 crore.
- Called a **"green budget"**: about 21% (≈ ₹21,600–22,200 crore) for environment and pollution control.
- Education ≈ ₹19,326 crore; health ≈ ₹13,034 crore; transport ₹8,374 crore; women and child development ₹7,406 crore (₹406 crore for free bus travel for women).
- ₹1,352 crore for dust-free roads; ₹1,000 crore to MCD for roads; free bicycles to 1.3 lakh girl students.
