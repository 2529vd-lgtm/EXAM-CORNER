### December 2018

**Q1.** Which of the following statements about rational expectations is false? *[December 2018 · Q.62]*

(1) It assumes that people take account of all information.  
(2) It assumes that on an average the forecasts made by the public are correct.  
(3) It implies that the expected changes in demand have no effect on unemployment.  
(4) It implies that unexpected changes in demand have long lasting effect on both unemployment and price level.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(4)** It implies that unexpected changes in demand have long lasting effect on both unemployment and price level.  
Under rational expectations unexpected demand changes affect output only temporarily, not for long.

</details>

**Q2.** Which amongst the following is/are correct for the Keynesian model? *[December 2018 · Q.64]*

(1) It assumes full employment of resources.  
(2) It ignores the money market.  
(3) It is supply determined.  
(4) None of the above

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(4)** None of the above  
The Keynesian model allows under-employment, includes the money market and is demand-determined.

</details>

**Q5.** An increase in the level of government spending, other things being equal, will in the IS-LM
